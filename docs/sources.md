# Sources and licences

## Study material and questions

The app quotes or extracts its content from these publications. Each is credited in the app.
Copyright stays with each publisher.

| Source | Publisher | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024 | Department of Telecommunications, Government of India (Gazette of India) | Rules notes, licence info, fees |
| ASOC User Manual (application portal) | Department of Telecommunications eServices | How to apply |
| NIAR Study Manual (2021 edition) | National Institute of Amateur Radio, Hyderabad | Study notes, reference tables, hints |
| NIAR Study Manual (2016 edition) | National Institute of Amateur Radio, Hyderabad | Cross-reference |
| Amateur Radio Study Material (2020) | Sandeep Baruah, VU2MUE | Exam questions with answer key |
| ASOC Exam Question Paper with Answers, Section B | RPC Ham Radio Club, Ranganathan Polytechnic College, Coimbatore | Section B questions with answer key |
| NCVEC question pools (USA) | National Conference of Volunteer Examiner Coordinators, public domain | Cross-checking answer keys |
