# FAQ

??? question "Is this the official exam app?"
    No. ASOC Exam Prep is an independent, free study aid. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by
    the Department of Telecommunications, WPC or NIAR. Check the official rules before your exam.

??? question "Are the exam questions the real ones?"
    They come from published ASOC question banks, the VU2MUE study material and the RPC Ham Radio
    Club Section B paper, with the answers from their **published keys**. Nothing was written
    for the app. Questions whose published keys contradicted each other were left out. Each
    question shows its source after you answer.

??? question "Why do only some questions have hints?"
    Hints are passages copied word for word from the study notes. If the notes don't contain a
    passage that clearly explains a question, it gets no hint rather than an invented one. You
    still get a link to that topic's notes.

??? question "Which grade should I choose?"
    The **Restricted** grade has an easier pass mark and no Morse test, but lower power limits.
    The **General** grade needs the Morse code test at 8 WPM. See
    [First steps](first-steps.md#choose-your-grade) and **Study → Licence info**.

??? question "I can't hear any Morse or phonetics."
    Turn up the **media** volume (not the ringer), check the phone isn't connected to a Bluetooth
    device you can't hear, and make sure **Do Not Disturb** isn't muting media.

??? question "My sending is decoded wrongly."
    Keep dots short and dashes about three times as long. Leave a clear gap between letters and a
    longer one between words. The decoder adapts to your speed after a few characters; tap
    **Clear** and try again. See [Send practice](morse.md#send-practice).

??? question "Can I use it without internet?"
    Yes. Everything is inside the app; it doesn't use the internet at all.

??? question "How do I move my progress to a new phone?"
    Progress is stored only on the phone, and there's no export yet.

??? question "Android says the app can't be installed."
    If you have an older test build, uninstall it first; see [Install](install.md#updating). Also
    check you're on Android 8.0 or newer and have allowed installs from your browser or Files app.

??? question "I found a wrong answer or a bug."
    Please [open an issue](https://github.com/ClaudeShaji/asoc-exam-prep/issues) with the question
    text, or a screenshot and the steps to reproduce.
