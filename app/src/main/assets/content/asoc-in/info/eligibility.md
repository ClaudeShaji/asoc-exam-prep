# Who can apply

- Citizen of India
- At least **12 years** old
- Must pass the Amateur Station Operator examination

There are two certificates: **ASOC (General)** and **ASOC (Restricted)**. General allows more emission types and higher power.

## Foreign nationals

The Central Government may allow a non-citizen to take the exam, subject to administrative clearance. A foreigner holding a similar certificate from their own country may apply for an ASOC (fee ₹1,000), subject to Ministry of External Affairs clearance. That certificate lasts for the visa period or 12 months, whichever is shorter.

*Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024, rules 3 and 4.*
