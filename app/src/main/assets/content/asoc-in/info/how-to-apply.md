# How to apply

Applications are online on the Department of Telecommunications portal (Saral Sanchar, linked from the ASOC page on eservices.dot.gov.in).

1. **Register** as *Individual (WPC Exams/Certificates)*. Verify email and mobile by OTP; login details arrive by email.
2. **Log in**, change the password, and complete *Update Personal Details*.
3. **Apply for the exam**: Apply for Examinations → New Applications → Amateur Station Exam.
4. Fill in applicant details, ID and address proofs, then choose the **grade** and **exam centre**.
5. Pay the **₹100** exam fee and enter the payment details and receipt.
6. **Validate** and **submit**.
7. After scrutiny a **hall ticket** is issued; the date and venue come by SMS.

Apply at least **one month** before the exam date.

## Upload limits

- PDF documents under 1 MB
- Photograph under 250 KB
- Signature under 150 KB

## Documents

- Photo ID / nationality proof
- Present and permanent address proof
