# After passing

1. **Apply for the certificate** on the portal (Apply for Certificates → Amateur → Exam conducted through this portal). Give the station location, equipment details and address proof.
2. **Pay** the certificate fee within **2 years** of the result, or you must take the exam again.
3. The certificate is issued with your **unique call sign**.

## Validity and renewal

- 20 years, or lifetime (until age 80)
- Renew at least **12 months** before expiry; up to 2 years late with a ₹1,000 late fee; not after that
- Lifetime certificates can be extended 10 years at a time at no fee
- No separate import licence is needed for amateur radio equipment

## Clubs and events

- 4 or more ASOC holders can form an **amateur society** with its own call sign (valid 20 years or the custodian's ASOC, whichever is shorter)
- A **special event call sign** can be requested 90 days ahead; valid 90 days

## Duties

- Provide radio communication free of charge when the Central or State Government asks, during disasters
- Produce records and equipment for inspection when asked

*Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024, rules 6–14.*
