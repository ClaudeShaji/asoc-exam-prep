# Exam pattern

The exam is in **English**. One written paper with two sections of **50 multiple-choice questions** each, equal marks:

- **Section A** — Radio Theory and Practice
- **Section B** — Radio Regulations

Engineering or science graduates and engineering diploma holders who studied electronics or telecommunications are exempt from Section A.

## Restricted

- 1 hour, 100 marks
- At least **40%** in each section and **50%** overall
- No Morse test

## General

- 2 hours, 100 marks
- At least **50%** in each section and **60%** overall
- **Morse** receiving and sending at **8 words per minute**

## Morse test (General)

- 5 minutes of plain text, about 200 characters (letters, figures, full stop, comma, semicolon, break sign, hyphen, question mark)
- Figures and punctuation count as two characters
- One-minute practice piece first; one attempt only
- More than **5 errors** fails; one error-free minute passes
- Receiving and sending must both be passed

## Note

The 2024 Rules (rule 5(5)) only state a minimum of 40% of total marks; the syllabus marks above are stricter. The Government publishes the syllabus, venue and dates on the portal for each exam.
