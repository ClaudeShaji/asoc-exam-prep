# Fees

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Exam | ₹100 |
| Certificate, 20 years | ₹1,000 |
| Certificate, lifetime (until age 80) | ₹2,000 |
| Late renewal (up to 2 years after expiry) | ₹1,000 extra |
| Duplicate certificate or change of station location | ₹1,000 |
| Amateur society (4 or more ASOC holders) | ₹2,000 |
| Special event call sign | ₹200 |
| Foreign certificate holder applying for ASOC | ₹1,000 |

The same fees apply to General and Restricted. No refund on suspension, cancellation or surrender.

*Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024.*
