# Operating your station

## Messages

- Only with other authorised amateur stations
- Plain language, technical in nature
- **Not allowed**: broadcasts, music or entertainment, false calls, news, advertising, business, political or industrial controversy, third-party messages, offensive language
- In disasters, third-party messages from a District Magistrate or other competent authority may be permitted

## Identification

- Morse: call sign after **DE**; voice: after **THIS IS**
- End a message with **AR** (Morse) or **OVER** (voice)
- End the contact with **VA** (Morse) or **OUT** (voice)

## Technical

- Stay inside the authorised bands, emissions and power for your grade
- Keep a reliable frequency measuring device at the station
- Cause no interference; stop or reduce transmission if asked
- Damped-wave emissions are not allowed

## Log book

- Bound book, not loose-leaf; no gaps; written at the time
- Date and time (**IST**), summary, experiments, stations worked with time and emission, opening and closing times, temporary location if portable or mobile
- Keep for **one year** after the last entry

## Equipment

- The station must be able to receive as well as transmit
- Keep it safe and away from unauthorised people
- Keep a register of equipment: make, model, serial number, source, date

*Telecommunications (Amateur Services) Rules, 2024, Annexure A.*
