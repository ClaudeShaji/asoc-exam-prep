Voice recordings of the NATO/ITU spelling-alphabet words (a-z), the digits (d0-d9) and "niner".
Recorded by Allison Smith for the Asterisk open-source PBX ("asterisk-core-sounds-en", G.722 set,
https://downloads.asterisk.org/pub/telephony/sounds/), files phonetic/*_p and digits/0-9.
